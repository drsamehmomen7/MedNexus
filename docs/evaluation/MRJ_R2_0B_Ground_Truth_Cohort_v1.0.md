## Section-aware annotation method

**Summary-first, whole-report-aware:** The Diagnostic Summary identifies the principal clinical message; the Findings/Description provides evidence and detail that enriches that message. MRJ preserves multi-section provenance and does not treat all report sections as equally salient. Reviewers identify governed semantic section roles, assign independent clinical salience and assertion, and link multiple exact `EVIDENCE_ANCHORS` to one fact or its specific attributes instead of duplicating a clinical object across sections. Source-supported normal/negative observations remain eligible. Material summary/body disagreement is preserved as `INTERNAL_REPORT_CONFLICT` for human adjudication. Human review accepted this section-aware method, including canonical section roles, Clinical Salience, multi-section Evidence Anchors, and conflict handling. The external workbook, schema, guidelines, coverage template, and span validator support it; clinical annotation and engine evaluation have not started, and no clinical EXTRACT engine is implemented.
