# R2.0C Clinical Synthesis Contract and EXTRACT Experience

**Status: ACCEPTED / CLOSED — 4 October 2026.** R2.0C Clinical Synthesis and Cross-Radiology Generalization are accepted for horizontal journey progression. The EXTRACT UX is accepted as a working clinical shell; the final Unified Journey visual system is deferred. This milestone does not claim universal clinical accuracy or alter the accepted R2.0A fact contract or R2.0B ground-truth framework.

## Clinical synthesis authority

The RadGraph-XL result and the existing MRJ-validated `radiology_findings`, relationships, context facts, measurements, candidates, and review candidates remain available in the EXTRACT response. `raw_engine_evidence` retains the worker sections and every grounded engine candidate. The R2.0C `clinical_synthesis` object is an additional, deterministic presentation layer. It uses exact protected-text anchors. Validated clinical observations enter an evidence-first candidate decision without requiring a named disease rule; explicit suppression removes administrative text, isolated attributes, unsupported fragments, and routine normal observations. Existing named mappings remain as terminology/display normalization for the accepted CT reference, not as the only route to canonicalization. A COMPARISON anchor can qualify an already grounded current focal finding as chronic, but cannot introduce a historical lesion as a new current fact.

Each `canonical_fact` has a stable within-result ID, type, concept, display label, assertion, salience, source-specific observations, exact evidence anchors, contributing engine evidence IDs, provenance, and targeted review reasons. Missing attributes are omitted. Compatible Findings and Impression mentions merge while their section roles and anchors remain separate. Opposing assertions remain separate with a conflict review reason. Measurements and lesion counts stay attached to observations with their source acquisition context. Diagnostic hypotheses are represented with `UNCERTAIN` assertion and a source-supported status such as `FAVORED`, `POSSIBLE`, `LESS_FAVORED`, or `LESS_LIKELY`; they never become present findings. A pilot-specific, exactly grounded chronic focal white-matter mention held by the prior broad temporal firewall can enter synthesis with explicit temporal information. Unsupported generic entities and modifiers remain in technical evidence.

`clinical_synthesis.clinical_synthesis` is assembled from accepted canonical fact labels and bounded phrases. It is not generative. No causal relation, diagnosis confirmation, terminology code, or missing demographic is inferred. The pilot profile deliberately abstains on unsupported or incomplete source fragments. Overall EXTRACT can remain `NEEDS_REVIEW` while only individual facts with diagnostic interpretation, source-specific lesion counts, or conflicts receive targeted fact warnings. `technical_diagnostics` records grounded evidence, candidate, canonical fact, suppressed candidate, and merged-mention counts, with per-candidate decisions and rejection reasons. A `SYNTHESIS_COVERAGE_FAILURE` code and review reason mark a zero-fact synthesis when clinically grounded engine evidence exists; a zero result is not silently represented as ordinary success.

## Prior functional generalization gate — insufficient for broad Radiology

Functional abnormalities are eligible clinical findings when an exact, protected Findings or Impression anchor supports the abnormal state. An Impression-supported non-lesion condition can establish the principal fact. Source-explicit functional effects and associations retain their evidence-linked relationships; absent normal function is a present abnormality, while explicit absence of a pathology remains a pertinent negative. Clinical history remains context and is not promoted to a current finding. Generic modifiers and anatomy alone remain in technical evidence. The generalization uses reusable condition and functional-state cues, without report IDs, modality-specific branches, or anatomy-specific output rules. Generic negatives are introduced conservatively when the bounded profile otherwise has no clinical facts; source-grounded functional positives may augment an existing profile.

The protected US_X-ray_04 end-to-end run retained 27 validated engine findings and synthesized 9 canonical facts: 6 key findings and 3 pertinent negatives, plus a separate history context. Synthetic Doppler and thyroid ultrasound checks produced 3 and 1 canonical facts respectively. A fresh US_CT_07 run retained all 16 accepted facts and an identical synthesis and canonical projection. The later IN_MRI_02 failure showed that this two-report gate did not establish general Radiology coverage.

## R2.0C synthesis architecture correction — accepted for horizontal progression

The pre-correction audit found approximately 30–35 named clinical patterns influencing canonical selection: 16 `RULES` entries for neuro-CT conditions and hypotheses, 8 `FUNCTIONAL` entries added for swallowing/function reports, and 11 named `NEGATIVE_CONDITIONS` alternatives (with overlap). `_rule`, `_general_rule`, and the `bounded_active` negative-pertinence gate made this an effective growing whitelist. The named `RULES`, `_finish_fact`, and CT-specific `_summary` branches are now treated as legacy terminology/display normalization and accepted-reference compatibility; they do not define the default eligibility of an unfamiliar validated positive observation. `FUNCTIONAL` labels and source-explicit relation patterns remain targeted semantic normalization. `NEGATIVE_CONDITIONS` and the conservative negative-pertinence gate remain a known review limitation rather than a claim of universal pertinent-negative recall. The generic `NOISE`, `GENERIC_MODIFIERS`, `LOW_INFORMATION_HEAD`, `ISOLATED_ATTRIBUTE`, administrative, technique, and routine-normal patterns are explicit suppression/attribute rules. Contrast-context, hypothesis-status, Impression salience, and source-anchor checks are generic semantic rules. No filename, report ID, shoulder anatomy, Bankart, Hill-Sachs, or labral-tear branch is used for eligibility.

| Audited mechanism | Classification | Role after correction |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `RULES` (16) and `_finish_fact` concept labels | Domain-specific clinical rule / terminology normalization | Accepted CT display and equivalence only; unfamiliar positives use the open path. |
| `FUNCTIONAL` (8) and `_general_label` | Domain-specific clinical rule / terminology normalization | Normalize established functional wording and qualified negatives. |
| `NEGATIVE_CONDITIONS` (11 alternatives) and `bounded_active` | Domain-specific clinical rule | Conservative pertinent-negative selection; residual limitation for review. |
| `NOISE`, `GENERIC_MODIFIERS`, generic-head, attribute, administrative, technique, and normal patterns | Suppression rule | Reject or demote low-information evidence with a recorded reason. |
| Contrast-context, `_hypothesis_status`, assertion separation, Impression salience, source-span and cross-section corroboration | Generic semantic rule | Establish provenance, uncertainty, and role without a disease-name gate. |
| Lesion-oriented `_summary` and exact accepted CT display phrases | Report-specific / overfit legacy presentation | Preserved for the reference projection; not used as basic eligibility. |

Each exactly grounded validated Observation receives a local source-function and semantic-role decision with a source span, final acceptance or rejection, and a reason. An unfamiliar grounded condition can therefore survive without appearing in any named concept table. A source-only missed assertion can be recovered when a protected source clause explicitly supports it under bounded recovery rules; cross-section corroboration retains two exact anchors where available. Assertions (`PRESENT`, `ABSENT_NEGATED`, `UNCERTAIN`), hypothesis status, and semantic role remain separate. An explicit muscular atrophy negative cannot inherit the legacy “Cerebral atrophy” display label. Measurements and extent attach to their source-supported fact; source-explicit relations are retained. Administrative disclaimers, normal procedure maneuvers, bare modifiers, generic nouns, and incomplete fragments remain technical evidence.

The zero-fact safety gate emits `SYNTHESIS_COVERAGE_FAILURE` in `technical_diagnostics.code` and `review_summary.reasons` whenever clinically grounded validated evidence exists but no canonical fact is produced. Technical diagnostics include validated/clinically grounded evidence, synthesis candidates, accepted facts, suppressed candidates, merged mentions, rejection-reason counts, and per-candidate decisions. These are debugging/Technical Evidence data, not a new clinician-facing card or a clinical acceptance claim.

US_CT_07 remains the exact 16-fact accepted projection (3 key, 4 diagnostic, 9 secondary). US_X-ray_04 remains 9 facts (6 key, 3 pertinent negatives) with history context. IN_MRI_02 yields six grounded major findings covering labral tear, Bankart and Hill-Sachs lesions, coracoid fracture, glenohumeral ligament tears, and joint effusion; source-specific dimensions attach to the Hill-Sachs fact and Sports injury remains context. These outputs remain `NEEDS_REVIEW` at report level, so individual clinical results still require human review before downstream analytics.

The additional protected synthetic survey covered different anatomy, modality, and style: US_CT_01 (24 validated findings, 14 canonical facts: 6 key, 2 diagnostic, 4 secondary, 2 pertinent negatives) retained lung/pleural, liver, lymph-node, and hiatal-hernia content; US_Doppler_03 (16 validated, 3 canonical: one mass and two uncertain diagnostic considerations) kept the mass separate from its possible interpretations; IN_X-ray_06 (16 validated, one pertinent negative) suppressed report-disclaimer and routine-normal text. No obvious administrative text or isolated modifier was promoted in those final runs. This survey is a generalization check, not clinical acceptance or a claim of exhaustive extraction.

## Clause-Level Source Function Correction — accepted architecture

Broad Findings, Impression, and Comparison section labels establish context but cannot authorize every phrase inside the region as a patient finding. Synthesis now evaluates each exactly grounded local clause before a named terminology rule, functional rule, or open-vocabulary rule can create a canonical fact. The processing order is **validated evidence → local source-function classification → assertion, uncertainty, and temporal interpretation → semantic role → explicit eligibility/suppression → attribute and relation attachment → salience → canonical fact**. This preserves evidence-first basic eligibility without an anatomy, modality, report-template, or condition whitelist.

The local classifier separates patient findings and interpretations from questionnaire prompts, recommendations, educational/reference text, technique and procedure descriptions, device context, visibility limitations, prior-only comparisons, technical attributes, and incomplete fragments. Lowercase PDF line continuations retain the modifier or comparison qualifier with their clinical head; numbered patient-specific Impression statements remain distinct from generic numbered reference lists. A current finding or interval change within a Comparison section remains eligible. A historical statement alone does not become current. Interpretive cues such as “suggestive of” and “likely” retain uncertain diagnostic status. Generic headed patient states can be recovered from exact source clauses when the engine captured only a modifier; an explicitly equivalent summary state keeps the source measurement and anchor without a duplicate fact. Measured conditions remain observations, with measurements attached as attributes rather than separate facts. Source-only assessment recovery is confined to a concise Impression clause and excludes adjacent advice. A validated secondary observation can gain Key salience only when a patient-specific Impression clause corroborates its distinctive clinical head. Conflicting patient-specific classifications remain separate and carry `INTERNAL_SOURCE_CONFLICT` for review.

The protected source clause, broad section role, local source function, eligibility decision, and rejection reason remain in technical diagnostics and provenance. Recommendations and other rejected text remain available through protected evidence rather than clinician-facing Key Findings. The existing zero-fact `SYNTHESIS_COVERAGE_FAILURE` gate is unchanged. The accepted processing order is **validated evidence → local clause source function → assertion / uncertainty / temporal interpretation → semantic role → evidence-first eligibility → explicit suppression → attribute / relation attachment → salience → canonical clinical fact**. A model mention is not itself a canonical fact. Synthesis remains summary-first and whole-report-aware while retaining multiple exact anchors, source-specific observations, diagnostic hypotheses, conflicts, and provenance.

## Horizontal platform and record direction

**One platform, one Journey, multiple domain intelligence packs.** Radiology and Public Health are domains in the same MRJ platform. A future common layer should govern the Journey shell, report lifecycle, review workflow, processing states, batch/collection mechanics, navigation, provenance, human-reviewed record gating, analytics eligibility, and visual identity. The present EXTRACT shell is not the final Unified Journey redesign; that work waits until all seven stages are functionally connected.

Every report should ultimately have **Common Report Context / Metadata** plus a **Domain-Specific Structured Record**. Common context conceptually includes report/document ID, domain, document type, source, language, encounter/service date when available, safe patient analytic context after PROTECT, processing status, provenance, confidence, review state, and collection/batch membership. This is direction only; no large shared schema is introduced by R2.0C closure.

The conceptual Radiology structured record comprises Study Context; Clinical Indication; Principal Findings; Associated Findings; Anatomy / Laterality; Measurements / Quantification; Diagnostic Considerations; Temporal / Comparison Change; Secondary / Incidental Findings; Pertinent Negatives / Relevant Normal Findings; explicitly stated Recommendation / Follow-up; and Evidence / Human Review / Provenance. Not every report populates every slot, and missing does not mean false.

The cross-domain analytics gate is **AI Evidence → Structured Domain Record → Human Review → Saved / Accepted Record → Eligible for Collection Analytics → ANALYZE → VISUALIZE → INDICATORS**. Dashboards and indicators should consume reviewed, eligible records rather than uncontrolled raw engine output. This workflow is documented, not implemented here.

The next milestone is **R3.0A — STANDARDIZE V0**. STANDARDIZE consumes accepted EXTRACT canonical facts and transforms a canonical clinical fact into a governed standardized clinical concept; it must not rediscover findings. Potential reference systems include RadLex, SNOMED CT, LOINC / RSNA Radiology Playbook, and UCUM. No terminology implementation is part of this closure.
